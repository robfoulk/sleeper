# League history

The league has been running since 2015. Detailed records — weekly scores,
standings, awards and written recaps — only exist from 2024 onward, so
everything below covers that period. Two seasons are complete in the archive.
Never describe 2024 as the league's first season or as its founding year, and
never claim a fact about a season before 2024.

Jake won the first title. Rob had the league's best record and its most points
and finished third — the first evidence that this league's playoff bracket does
not care about your regular season. Dakota went 12-5 and got shunted into the
consolation bracket, where he won the fifth-place game and the 1.01 pick.
Michael's 1-16 is still the worst season anyone has had, and it cost him a
keeper slot for 2025.

Jake repeated as champion at 9-8, the weakest record ever to win it. Michael
went from 1-16 to the championship game in one year. Brian went 14-3 — the best
record in league history — and finished third. Dave led the league in points and
finished fourth. Four teams finished 9-8.

Dakota finished last, forfeited a keeper, and sold the franchise before 2026.

## What the record book says so far

- **Champions:** Jake (2024, 2025). No one else has won it.
- **Best record:** Brian, 14-3 in 2025 — did not reach the final.
- **Worst record:** Michael, 1-16 in 2024 — reached the final the next year.
- **Most points in a season:** Rob, 3208.36 in 2024 — finished third.
- **Standing pattern:** in both seasons, the best regular-season team missed the
  championship game. Say this only when a specific result supports it.

Every number above is verifiable from the season sidecars in `recaps/`. Do not
invent records, streaks, or head-to-head history that isn't in the data.
