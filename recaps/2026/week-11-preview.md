# Week 11 Preview: Six Teams Clock Out

_The League, 2026. Pairings, ownership, and current platform tags checked Tuesday, November 17. Upcoming lineups can still change._

Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Rams, New England, and Seattle are on bye. That's a lot of empty fantasy chairs, and every matchup has its own way to make them louder.

## Dave vs Eric: The Bye-Week Quarterback Test

**Early and out of hope (5-5) vs Boutte on Sunday (5-5).** Dave beat Jake last week; Eric lost to Michael. Eric's 1,783.08 points for lead Michael's 1,719.38 by 63.70; Dave sits at 1,632.96.

Dave's current roster loses Matthew Stafford and Packers receivers Christian Watson and Matthew Golden to bye weeks. His Vikings quarterback goes to San Francisco, Cam Skattebo hosts Jacksonville, and Rashee Rice gets Arizona. Travis Kelce's ankle is a fresh question: Sleeper lists him as questionable.

Eric can send C.J. Stroud against Indianapolis on Thursday and Joe Burrow to Washington on Monday. New England's bye and Drake Maye's significant ankle injury keep the newly acquired quarterback out of this week's plans; surgery and a return timeline remain unresolved. Eric also has Tetairoa McMillan against Baltimore, while Sleeper lists Carnell Tate out and Wan'Dale Robinson on injured reserve.

**Pick: Eric.** Two active quarterbacks give him more ways through the week. Dave has home matchups for Skattebo and Rashee Rice, though three bye slots could thin the field.

## Devin vs Jake: Four Byes Meet Two Out Tags

**Amon Another level (6-4) vs Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast (1-9).** Devin has the better record and three more wins; Jake gets a real opening from Devin's bye list.

Four of Devin's current starters are attached to Atlanta, Los Angeles, or Seattle: Bijan Robinson, Kyren Williams, Jaxon Smith-Njigba, and Jadarian Price. Dak Prescott hosts Tennessee, Amon-Ra St. Brown hosts Tampa Bay, and George Kittle gets Minnesota.

Jake's quarterbacks have playable home games: Bo Nix hosts Las Vegas, while Bryce Young gets Baltimore. Malik Nabers and the Giants host Jacksonville. Sleeper lists Ladd McConkey and Kyle Monangai out, with Luther Burden questionable; Woody Marks adds a Thursday question after his Week 10 concussion and Tuesday practice absence.

**Pick: Jake, in an upset.** Devin's core still has plenty of firepower, but four bye slots create a narrow path for Jake to make this far closer than the records suggest.

## Rob vs Michael: The New Gibbs Test

**Unstoppable Farce (9-1) vs Broken leg broken team (5-5).** Rob is coming off his first loss. Michael arrives with 235.86 points from Week 10 and a Buffalo pairing that should keep the scoreboard busy.

Rob now has Gibbs, whose Lions host Tampa Bay, plus Derrick Henry at Carolina, Trevor Lawrence at the Giants, and Ja'Marr Chase at Washington. The bye takes Kyle Pitts, Rhamondre Stevenson, and Zach Charbonnet out of his current starting group.

Michael's Josh Allen and James Cook host Miami. Jared Goff hosts Tampa Bay, and Jonathan Taylor visits Houston on Thursday. Justin Jefferson goes to San Francisco. His key options remain on the field while Rob patches three spots.

**Pick: Michael, in an upset.** Allen and Cook get the home matchup together, and Michael's last score proved the ceiling is real. Gibbs gives Rob a shiny new counterpunch; the bye list gives Michael the opening.

## Brian vs Travon: First Place Against the Long Climb

**Tri-Lams (8-2) vs Von (1-9).** Brian has the week's top score behind him and a chance to keep Rob within reach. Travon needs a win, and Patrick Mahomes against Arizona gives him a strong place to start.

Brian's Brock Purdy and Christian McCaffrey host Minnesota, while CeeDee Lamb hosts Tennessee. Jaylen Waddle is on the other side of a concern: Sleeper still lists him out with a head injury. Bucky Irving goes to Detroit.

Travon can start Mahomes against Arizona, Trey McBride at Kansas City, and Chris Olave at Chicago. Jordan Addison's thumb injury has moved from a missed game to injured reserve; Minnesota expects him back in about four weeks. Quinshon Judkins also loses the week to Cleveland's bye.

**Pick: Brian.** Purdy, McCaffrey, and Lamb give him a sturdy route to another big week. McBride can make the Kansas City-Arizona game uncomfortable for Brian's tight end plans, but Addison's absence takes one of Travon's best receivers out of the picture.
