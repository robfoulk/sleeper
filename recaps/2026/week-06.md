## Around the League: The Waiver Claim Becomes Infrastructure

Michael dropped Drake Maye on September 30. Rob claimed him on October 2. Maye has since supplied **39.46** in Rob's Week 5 lineup and **23.80** in Week 6. With Hurts expected to miss the remainder of 2026, that claim has become part of the season's foundation.

Rob also added Kyle Pitts before Week 5; Pitts followed his 10.20 debut in that lineup with **18.00** this week. Devin's October 16 addition of Huntley yielded **13.40** in Week 6. Dave added Andy Borregales on Sunday and received **7.00**. The small transactions have become part of the weekly scoring story.

The injury news is more consequential than any single waiver return:

- **Travon loses Terry McLaurin for the season.** Washington confirmed Tuesday that he tore his patellar tendon in Monday's game. His 2.70 remained part of Travon's Week 6 total; the larger loss is everything that would have followed.
- **Rob has three players on IR, with two expected to miss the rest of the season.** NFL Network and ESPN report that Hurts is expected to miss the remainder of 2026 while receiving treatment for blood clots. The Eagles and his agent say his prognosis is very good and they expect a full recovery. Grupe will miss the rest of the season with a knee injury. Adams' expected **six-to-ten-week** hamstring absence is attributed to Sean McVay in NFL reporting.
- **Dave loses Romeo Doubs to injured reserve.** New England announced the completed transaction Monday, following his Week 6 shoulder injury.
- **Devin has another Mike Evans question.** Evans aggravated his Achilles issue Monday. Sleeper lists him out; Kyle Shanahan's postgame comments left open a possible return at Atlanta without guaranteeing it. His Sunday availability remains unresolved.
- **Jake must monitor Ladd McConkey.** Sunday's concussion ended his game. The Chargers' Week 7 bye gives him more time; his next appearance depends on medical clearance.

The weekly injury ledger covers **231 of 240** Week 6 rostered players. Its **42 source-specific entries across 34 players** include 14 recoveries and 28 changes with uncertain timing. Those entries describe available evidence; their timestamps do not establish when every injury began.
