# Week 10 Preview: Brian Takes His Shot

_The League, 2026. Filed Tuesday, November 10. Pairings and rosters checked around 11:32 p.m. Eastern; reporting through 11:36 p.m._

Rob is **9-0**. Brian is **7-2** and just scored **243.16**. They meet with first place in sight and a long list of lineup repairs on both sides.

**Chicago, Denver, Tampa Bay, and Philadelphia are on bye.** Washington visits the Giants at **8:15 p.m. Eastern Thursday**. New England and Detroit play in Munich at **9:30 a.m. Eastern Sunday**; Maye, Goff, St. Brown, and Gibbs give four managers a stake in that early game.

## Rob vs Brian: The Chase Reaches the Leader

**Unstoppable Farce (9-0) vs Tri-Lams (7-2).** Rob won their Week 3 meeting **202.82-142.22**.

Drake Maye faces Detroit in Munich and Trevor Lawrence visits Tennessee. Kenneth Walker goes to Atlanta, Jaylen Warren returns from Pittsburgh's bye to face Cincinnati, Ja'Marr Chase hosts the Steelers, and Brock Bowers gets Seattle. Rob's depth has survived nine weeks of questions; it has a few more this time.

Josh Jacobs needs finger surgery, with his return date unresolved. Rhamondre Stevenson left Week 9 with a knee injury. Sleeper lists both out, along with receivers Garrett Wilson and George Pickens. Colston Loveland's Chicago bye creates another opening.

Brian can pair Jordan Love against Minnesota with Brock Purdy at Dallas. Christian McCaffrey joins Purdy in that game; CeeDee Lamb plays on the other side, and Nico Collins visits Cleveland. Bucky Irving's Tampa Bay bye removes **24.50** from last week's winning lineup. Denver's bye sits Jaylen Waddle after his concussion, and Justin Herbert carries a questionable finger tag on Sleeper.

**Pick: Brian, in an upset.** Love, Purdy, McCaffrey, Lamb, and Collins give him enough paths to challenge the streak even with Irving away. Rob's Walker-Warren combination and the Maye-Lawrence pair make this a very uncomfortable opponent for a victory lap.

## Devin vs Travon: A New Quarterback in Baltimore

**Amon Another level (5-4) vs Von (1-8).** Devin won their Week 3 game **217.34-150.82**.

Devin has Dak Prescott against San Francisco and Geno Smith against Buffalo. Bijan Robinson hosts Kansas City, Amon-Ra St. Brown gets New England in Munich, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba visits Las Vegas. Lamar Jackson remains on injured reserve.

Travon's Patrick Mahomes visits Atlanta, though Reid called his elbow injury day-to-day. Jacoby Brissett has moved from Arizona to Baltimore, where the Ravens expect him to start against the Chargers on Monday night. Chris Olave hosts Carolina, Trey McBride faces the Rams, and Brian Thomas visits Tennessee. Travis Etienne remains on IR, David Montgomery has an out tag, and Chicago's bye takes Rome Odunze away.

**Pick: Devin.** Prescott, Robinson, and the receiving group offer more established production across the lineup. Travon's best answer starts with Mahomes feeling good enough to play and Brissett settling quickly into his new offense.

## Michael vs Eric: The First-Round Quarterback

**Broken leg broken team (4-5) vs Boutte on Sunday (5-4).** Eric took their Week 3 meeting **186.16-159.02**.

Michael can pair his Monday-night purchase, Josh Allen at the Jets, with Jared Goff against New England. James Cook also visits the Jets, Jonathan Taylor hosts Miami, and Justin Jefferson goes to Green Bay. Jake Ferguson's knee injury leaves a tight-end vacancy; Tyler Warren against Miami and Pat Freiermuth at Cincinnati are options.

Eric has three quarterbacks with useful matchups to consider: Jayden Daniels at the Giants on Thursday, C.J. Stroud at Cleveland, and Joe Burrow at home against Pittsburgh. Jahmyr Gibbs faces New England in Munich, Blake Corum visits Arizona, and Tetairoa McMillan goes to New Orleans.

**Pick: Eric, narrowly.** His quarterback choices and Gibbs give him a strong answer to Allen. Michael has won four of five, and the Cook-Taylor backfield can make that answer look insufficient.

## Jake vs Dave: Two New Quarterback Maps

**Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast (1-8) vs Early and out of hope (4-5).** Dave won their Week 3 meeting **189.46-150.70**.

Jake's first victory arrived just before Denver's bye takes away Bo Nix. Bryce Young visits New Orleans, and the Raiders have named Jake's Fernando Mendoza their starter against Seattle. Malik Nabers faces Washington on Thursday. Puka Nacua is likely headed to IR after his knee and toe injuries; Chicago's bye removes Luther Burden, and Philadelphia's sits Saquon Barkley. Sleeper lists Ladd McConkey questionable with a back issue.

Dave has Matthew Stafford at Arizona and Daniel Jones against Miami. His Kirk Cousins is headed from Las Vegas to Minnesota in a trade the Vikings announced as pending a physical; that announcement didn't name a Week 10 starter. The quarterback's departure opened the Raiders' job for Jake's Mendoza. Christian Watson and Matthew Golden face the Vikings, Travis Kelce visits Atlanta, and Omarion Hampton goes to Baltimore. D'Andre Swift is on Chicago's bye, while Michael Wilson is listed on IR.

**Pick: Dave.** Stafford and Jones give him two quarterback options, and Watson, Golden, and Kelce form a useful supporting cast. Jake's route depends on Young, Mendoza, and Nabers making up for the absent stars; Mendoza's first NFL start makes that a fascinating gamble.
