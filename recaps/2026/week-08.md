# Week 8 Recap: The Bench Has a Complaint

_The League, 2026. Filed November 5. Week 8 ran October 29-November 2; fantasy results and rosters were checked Thursday around noon Eastern, with injury reporting through 12:15 p.m._

**Rob is 8-0. Jake is 0-8.** One quarterback on Jake's bench could have changed both records.

Eric took the weekly scoring title by half a point over Rob and survived a Monday challenge from Devin. Michael ended Brian's winning streak, and Dave finally put a stop to his own losing streak. Jake and Brian can compare notes about the quarterbacks they left on the bench.

## Rob vs Jake: A 0.92-Point Escape

Rob beat Jake **200.16-183.20**. Jake had the fourth-best score of the week; unfortunately, Rob had the second-best, which has become a familiar problem for the rest of the league.

Brock Bowers scored **21.20**, Jaylen Warren **20.30**, and Ja'Marr Chase **20.10** for Rob. Drake Maye and Trevor Lawrence supplied another **41.86** from the quarterback spots. Rob has now finished among the top three weekly scorers in all eight weeks.

Jake got **19.90** from Kyler Murray and **16.40** from Jonah Coleman, but Baker Mayfield finished at **1.40**. Bo Nix scored **19.28** on the bench. Swap those two and Jake wins **201.08-200.16**, by **0.92**. The two-time defending champion found a way to beat the undefeated team; he just didn't actually use it.

Luther Burden's **11.60** on Monday outscored Rob's remaining Colston Loveland by **7.10**, but Rob's Sunday lead held.

## Eric vs Devin: The Monday Chase

Eric beat Devin **200.66-189.70**. Joe Burrow's **39.48**, Jahmyr Gibbs' **22.80**, and Jacksonville's **20.00** on defense put Eric on top of the weekly scoring pile and pulled him level with Devin at **5-3**. Eric has won three straight.

Devin got **27.50** from Tee Higgins and **25.70** from Tony Pollard. He also lost Lamar Jackson on Baltimore's first offensive snap; Jackson's right-thumb injury left that quarterback slot at zero. The Ravens have ruled him out for Thursday, and their coach said surgery and an injured-reserve move were planned.

Devin began Monday **28.76** behind with Jaxon Smith-Njigba left and nobody remaining for Eric. Smith-Njigba delivered **17.80**. A good night, a genuine chase, and a scoring title still headed to Eric.

## Michael vs Brian: The Bench Had an Answer

Michael beat Brian **165.50-141.02**. Bhayshul Tuten returned from his bye with **27.90**; Braelon Allen added **21.30**, Chuba Hubbard **17.90**, and James Cook **16.10**. Michael's running backs did enough to overcome a quiet quarterback day and end Brian's four-game winning streak.

Brian had production from Jordan Love (**21.52**) and Bucky Irving (**21.00**), but Michael Penix finished at **-1.90** while Cam Ward's **26.26** sat on the bench. Starting Ward in that slot would have put Brian at **169.18**, ahead by **3.68**. The bench has now won an imaginary game for two managers. The standings remain stubbornly uninterested.

Michael's Deshaun Watson returned after a first-quarter hit against Pittsburgh. The Browns said he developed concussion symptoms Monday night and entered the protocol Tuesday, leaving his next start unresolved. Jake Ferguson also left Sunday's game with an ankle injury.

## Dave vs Travon: The Slide Stops

Dave beat Travon **177.24-115.00**, ending a four-game skid. Kirk Cousins scored **28.18**, Josh Allen **26.56**, and D'Andre Swift **25.90**. Michael Wilson chipped in **18.80**, and Green Bay's defense added **12.00**. Dave is back to **4-4** and can retire the emergency meeting about a fifth straight loss.

Travon got **25.44** from Patrick Mahomes and **13.20** from Trey McBride, but Jacoby Brissett managed **3.46** before Arizona benched him. Von has lost six straight.

Dave also lost rookie running back Jeremiyah Love to a right-ankle injury against Dallas. Arizona placed Love on injured reserve Tuesday; the official transaction is a bigger development than his **2.30** in this matchup.
