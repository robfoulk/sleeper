# Week 9 Preview: Thursday Night and a Madrid Wake-Up Call

_The League, 2026. Filed Thursday, November 5, before Jacksonville-Baltimore. Pairings and rosters checked around noon Eastern; injury reporting through 12:15 p.m._

Rob's unbeaten run gets another visit from Devin. Brian and Eric meet with second place in reach. Dave and Michael are separated by a win, while Jake and Travon have one combined win and a Week 2 rematch neither wants to waste.

**Pittsburgh and Tennessee are on bye.** Jacksonville visits Baltimore at **8:15 p.m. Eastern Thursday**; Cincinnati and Atlanta meet in Madrid at **9:30 a.m. Eastern Sunday**. There are several ways to forget a lineup this week, and the league has thoughtfully spread them across two continents.

## Rob vs Devin: The Rematch at the Top

**Unstoppable Farce (8-0) vs Amon Another level (5-3).** Rob won their Week 2 meeting **191.48-183.16**.

Rob has Thursday decisions on both sides of Jacksonville-Baltimore with Trevor Lawrence and Derrick Henry. Drake Maye hosts Green Bay, Ja'Marr Chase gets Atlanta in Madrid, and Josh Jacobs visits New England. Jaylen Warren is on bye. Sleeper lists Garrett Wilson with a concussion and George Pickens with a hamstring issue, both questionable; those receiver decisions need another look.

Devin can pair Dak Prescott at Indianapolis with Geno Smith at Kansas City. Lamar Jackson is out Thursday, and Tony Pollard and Pittsburgh's defense are on bye. Tee Higgins and Bijan Robinson meet in Madrid, while Jaxon Smith-Njigba hosts Arizona. Amon-Ra St. Brown was limited Wednesday with a rib injury; his Sunday availability remains open.

**I pick Rob, narrowly.** His quarterback options and receiving depth give him room to absorb the bye. Devin's receivers and Prescott are more than capable of ending the streak, especially if St. Brown is ready.

## Eric vs Brian: A New Quarterback in the Room

Brian's **6-2** Tri-Lams took the first meeting **170.98-131.22**. Eric's **5-3** Boutte on Sunday arrives with three straight wins.

Eric has Joe Burrow against Atlanta in Madrid and Jayden Daniels against the Rams. Jahmyr Gibbs visits Miami, and Blake Corum goes to Washington. Rico Dowdle's Pittsburgh bye creates a running-back opening; Jacksonville's defense gives Eric a Thursday choice.

Brian has Jordan Love at New England, Justin Herbert against Houston, and newly acquired Brock Purdy against Las Vegas. Nico Collins returns from his bye for that Chargers game, and CeeDee Lamb visits Indianapolis. Christian McCaffrey missed Wednesday's practice: the 49ers-supplied report lists a hamstring injury, while Sleeper calls it a calf issue. His Sunday status needs checking. Cam Ward is on bye.

**I pick Eric.** Burrow, Daniels, and Gibbs give him a strong starting core while Brian sorts out McCaffrey. Purdy and Collins make this a much less comfortable call than last week's box score suggests.

## Dave vs Michael: One Win Apart

Dave has a one-win cushion at **4-4** over Michael at **3-5**. Their Week 2 game went to Dave **176.20-157.72**.

Dave has Josh Allen at Minnesota on Monday, Matthew Stafford at Washington, and Kirk Cousins at San Francisco. D'Andre Swift hosts Tampa Bay and Omarion Hampton faces Houston. Michael Wilson visits Seattle; Arizona says he played through two broken ribs against Dallas and is still managing them. Sleeper lists him as questionable, along with Alvin Kamara's back concern.

Michael has Jared Goff at Miami and Tyler Shough against Cleveland while Deshaun Watson remains in concussion protocol. Bhayshul Tuten is officially questionable with ankle and foot injuries for Thursday and did not practice Wednesday. James Cook visits Minnesota, Jonathan Taylor hosts Dallas, and Tyler Allgeier goes to Seattle. Michael has alternatives if the Thursday decision goes badly.

**I pick Dave.** Allen and his other quarterback options give him the steadier setup. Michael's running backs can swing this quickly, particularly if Tuten is available.

## Jake vs Travon: The Second Chance

The only win between them belongs to Travon (**1-7**), who took the first meeting **189.86-113.06**. Jake's lineup has come a long way since that afternoon; his **0-8** record has declined to participate.

Jake has Bo Nix at Carolina and newly named Arizona starter Gardner Minshew at Seattle. Kyler Murray's hamstring is a Monday-night problem: on Thursday, ESPN's Adam Schefter reported the situation was trending toward Carson Wentz starting, but Minnesota had not issued a final ruling. Saquon Barkley carries a questionable hamstring tag against the Giants. Puka Nacua visits Washington and Malik Nabers returns to face Philadelphia. Pittsburgh's bye takes kicker Chris Boswell out, leaving Jake a second kicker spot to fill.

Travon has Patrick Mahomes against the Jets. His other rostered quarterback, Jacoby Brissett, lost Arizona's starting job to Minshew after Week 8; Brock Purdy is now with Brian. Brian Thomas visits Baltimore on Thursday, Chris Olave hosts Cleveland, and Trey McBride goes to Seattle. David Montgomery is listed out and Travis Etienne is on injured reserve.

**I pick Jake, narrowly.** Nix and Minshew give him two named starters, with Nacua and a returning Nabers in the mix. Mahomes, Thomas, and McBride give Travon plenty of ways to make the first win wait another week.
