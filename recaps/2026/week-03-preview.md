# Week 3 Preview: The Unbeaten Table Has One Fewer Chair

_The League, 2026. Filed September 22. Current rosters and opponents come from the dated Week 2-final/Week 3-ahead snapshot. Week 3 runs September 24-28. Lineups are provisional; picks are editorial entertainment, not probabilities._

Rob and Brian are both 2-0, and the schedule has decided that is quite enough sharing. Michael and Eric are both 0-2, so their game offers somebody a first win unless they manage to split the inconvenience. Devin and Travon meet in the middle. Dave visits Crazy J's, where the nameplate is confident and the record is still awaiting supporting documentation.

Last week's preview went **2-2**, missing both upset picks. The [Week 2 recap](/sleeper/2026/week-02/) contains the receipts. This week we are keeping the opinions and retiring the pretense that enthusiasm is a forecasting model.

## Rob vs Brian: Somebody Loses the Perfect Record

**Unstoppable Farce (2-0) vs Tri-Lams (2-0)**

Rob has scored 400.36 through two weeks; Brian has 379.52. That 20.84-point season edge earns Rob an argument, not a Week 3 head start. Davante Adams' 31.50 rescued Rob on Monday, while Brian's second win came with useful contributions spread across the lineup. One spectacular performance and a broad supporting cast are both legal ways to get here.

**Rob's case:** Jalen Hurts faces Chicago on Monday, Chase gets Pittsburgh, and Derrick Henry faces Dallas. Kenneth Walker goes to Miami and De'Von Achane faces Kansas City, giving Rob two interests in the same NFL game without pretending both automatically get the best version of it. Adams visits Denver after his closing performance against the Giants. Asking for another 31.50 is tempting. Budgeting it in advance is how the column gets into trouble.

**Brian's case:** Christian McCaffrey faces Arizona, CeeDee Lamb visits Baltimore, and Bucky Irving faces Minnesota. Jordan Love has Atlanta on Thursday, while Justin Herbert visits Buffalo. Brian can begin putting actual points on the board before Rob's Monday quarterback gets near the field. Michael Penix has also been announced as Atlanta's Thursday starter, offering Brian another rostered quarterback option, not a command to change his lineup.

**The availability hinge:** Caleb Williams is week-to-week with a hamstring injury. Rob has Tyler Shough and Trevor Lawrence among his alternatives; there is no reason to describe this as an empty quarterback room. Bowers' planned practice return is encouraging but not clearance. Brian still needs fresh reporting on Nico Collins and DJ Moore rather than assuming old out tags either carry forward or disappear.

**The pick: Brian, narrowly.** The case is a broader available lineup getting enough from McCaffrey and the quarterbacks, not a claim that Rob's stars suddenly stopped being stars. Rob has already made one difficult Monday chase work. This time the column is asking Brian to leave a less cooperative target.

## Devin vs Travon: Read the Roster, Not the Draft-Day Brochure

**Amon Another level (1-1) vs Von (1-1)**

Devin's current roster includes **Lamar Jackson**; Justin Jefferson is now with Michael. Those are current ownership facts, not permission to move their Week 2 points between teams. Devin also retains Dak Prescott and Geno Smith. Dart's knee injury is serious news, but it does not leave Devin without other quarterbacks.

**Devin's case:** Lamar faces Dallas and Dak faces Baltimore, so the same NFL game can supply both sides of his quarterback plan. Bijan Robinson opens Thursday against Green Bay. Amon-Ra St. Brown gets the Jets and Jaxon Smith-Njigba faces Washington. The receiving room still has plenty to say after Smith-Njigba's 33.50 and St. Brown's 26.20 last week, even with a different name on the Jefferson shelf.

**Travon's case:** Mahomes gets Miami, Purdy visits Arizona, and Jonathan Taylor faces Houston. Their combined 82.66 helped drive last week's 189.86. Trey McBride meets San Francisco on the other side of Purdy's game, while Chris Olave faces Las Vegas. Kyler Murray has been announced as Minnesota's Week 3 starter after clearing protocol, expanding Travon's options rather than retroactively changing his Week 2 lineup.

**The availability hinge:** Dart's evaluation continues; possible season-ending surgery is not a confirmed outcome. Goedert is expected to miss time, and Reed's treatment and return timeline remain unsettled. Devin has George Kittle and Dalton Schultz at tight end, so the story is how he uses his alternatives, not whether the platform has enough red labels to frighten a columnist.

**The pick: Devin.** Lamar and Dak are the new center of the argument, with Bijan and that receiver core providing enough support. Travon's quarterbacks just supplied an excellent reason to disagree. This is a lean, not a coronation.

## Michael vs Eric: The First-Win Department Is Open

**Broken leg broken team (0-2) vs Sanders Boutte on Sunday (0-2)**

The Week 2 preview picked both owners to win. They lost by a combined 58.24 points. We have therefore arranged for them to play each other, a development for which the schedule deserves the credit and the prediction desk desperately needs the protection.

**Michael's case:** the current quarterback group includes Drake Maye and Jared Goff, and Jefferson joins DeVonta Smith, Stefon Diggs, and DK Metcalf among his receivers. Goff faces the Jets, Jefferson faces Tampa Bay, and James Cook gets the Chargers. Breece Hall meets Detroit. This is a changed roster, not an invitation to write another paragraph about Michael needing Lamar to carry him.

**Eric's case:** Joe Burrow faces Pittsburgh, C.J. Stroud gets Indianapolis, and Gibbs meets the Jets. Drake London opens Thursday against Green Bay, with Tucker Kraft playing Atlanta in the same game. Eric can establish an early position before Michael's Sunday core answers. The roster has quarterback alternatives even with Jayden Daniels ruled out for Week 3.

**The availability hinge:** Daniels' absence is confirmed, with Mariota starting for Washington. Alec Pierce is expected to miss several weeks for Michael. Eric's Rico Dowdle carries a questionable toe tag in the snapshot; that is a reason to check the next report, not a confirmed Sunday absence. Neither owner gets a healthy lineup simply because the other is also 0-2.

**The pick: Michael.** Jefferson and Goff change the shape of the offense, and Cook plus Hall give the argument substance beyond a desperate appeal to the law of averages. Eric can win through Gibbs and better quarterback production. The column is backing Michael's new combination, while acknowledging last week's confidence did him no favors.

## Jake vs Dave: The Showroom Meets the Scoreboard

**Crazy J's Stable of Stars (0-2) vs Early and out of hope (2-0)**

Jake's team name promises inventory. Dave's promises despair. After two weeks, the owner advertising despair has two wins and the stable is still waiting for a winning ticket. Branding departments are not subject to the standings tiebreaker.

**Jake's case:** Jeanty gets New Orleans, McConkey faces Buffalo, and Nabers faces Tennessee if available. Bo Nix gets the Rams, Baker Mayfield visits Minnesota, and Bryce Young gets Cleveland, giving Jake choices at quarterback. Sam Darnold's anticipated return to some practice work belongs on the watch list, not in the confirmed-starters column. There is a rebound case here without requiring every injured player to return at once.

**Dave's case:** Josh Allen faces the Chargers after scoring 40.82, Stafford visits Denver after 26.98, and Kelce gets Miami. **Omarion Hampton is now on Dave's roster**, with Buffalo next. Jake's current roster instead includes Kaelon Black. This week's head-to-head comes with changed inventory; last week's starter list is not a substitute for checking who owns whom today.

**The availability hinge:** Puka's Week 3 status remains unresolved despite McVay's optimism. Nabers returned from his shoulder injury Monday, which is not a guarantee for Sunday. Saquon Barkley has a questionable arm tag ahead of Monday's Chicago game. Jake needs alternatives ready if those uncertainties survive into the weekend; no diagnosis or clearance is inferred from the snapshot. Dave's Jordan Mason is on IR.

**The pick: Dave.** Allen and Stafford provide the clearest foundation, and Jake has too many availability questions to make the rebound the default. No clearance sale is being alleged. The column simply wants to see the merchandise on the field before buying the upset.
