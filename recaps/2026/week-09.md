# Week 9 Recap: 243 Points and the 8:51 Trade

_The League, 2026. Filed November 10. Week 9 ran November 5-9; scores and rosters were checked Tuesday around 11:32 p.m. Eastern, with reporting through 11:36 p.m._

Brian scored **243.16**. Rob won again. Jake finally got his first win of the season. And while Michael and Dave were finishing their matchup Monday night, they traded the quarterback Dave was still using against Michael.

## Brian vs Eric: A Very Expensive 179.80

**Brian 243.16, Eric 179.80.** Brian improves to **7-2** and stays two wins behind Rob.

Jordan Love scored **36.18** and Brock Purdy **25.28**. Bucky Irving added **24.50**, CeeDee Lamb **23.50**, and Christian McCaffrey **20.70**. Purdy's first start for Brian after the trade with Travon went rather well.

Brian's total is the **fifth-highest regular-season score in the records since 2024**. Eric's **179.80** ranked third this week and would have beaten five other opponents. Against Brian, it lost by **63.36**.

Joe Burrow supplied **29.54** for Eric. C.J. Stroud's **35.36** sat on the bench; replacing Jayden Daniels' **14.56** with it would have brought Eric to **200.60**. Brian's winning margin would still have been **42.56**.

Brian's Jaylen Waddle left Denver's game with a concussion. Denver has a Week 10 bye; his return after it remains subject to recovery and clearance.

## Rob vs Devin: Nine and Counting

Rob beat Devin **182.52-138.30**. Kenneth Walker III led the way with **32.30**, Ja'Marr Chase scored **21.50**, and Javonte Williams added **18.40**. Rob is **9-0**; every weekly scoring finish has landed in the top three.

Devin got **20.70** from Bijan Robinson, **19.50** from Dak Prescott, and **15.20** from Tee Higgins. The rest of the lineup couldn't keep pace, and Amon Another level falls to **5-4**.

Rob won despite getting only **0.50** from Josh Jacobs, who fractured a finger early against New England. Green Bay says Jacobs needs surgery and hasn't established a return timeline. Rhamondre Stevenson scored **11.80** before leaving with a knee injury, leaving Rob to watch two running backs ahead of the meeting with Brian.

## Michael vs Dave: Sold During the Show

Michael beat Dave **165.70-153.68** for his fourth win in five games after an **0-4** start. Tyler Shough scored **19.70**, James Cook **16.70**, and both Cam Little and Chicago's defense supplied **15.00**. Dave got **23.10** from Christian Watson and **22.40** from Matthew Golden.

Michael led **143.20-136.62** entering Monday. Cook and Justin Jefferson added **22.50** for him; Josh Allen and Jordan Mason answered with **17.06** for Dave.

At **8:51 p.m. Eastern Monday**, after Buffalo-Minnesota had kicked off, Dave traded Allen to Michael for Michael's **2027 first-round pick**. Allen's **12.86** counted for Dave in Week 9. He is now on Michael's roster for Week 10.

The two managers finish **4-5**. Michael got the win and Allen; Dave got a first-round pick and Allen's final shift in his lineup. For the rest of the night, Michael's newest quarterback was still working for the opposition.

Michael also lost tight end Jake Ferguson to a knee injury during Dallas' game. Cowboys coach Brian Schottenheimer expects Ferguson to miss multiple weeks.

## Jake vs Travon: Jake Gets His First

Jake beat Travon **153.42-107.82**, ending his **0-8** start. Puka Nacua scored **21.00**, Bryce Young **20.34**, Bo Nix **15.68**, and Luther Burden **13.70**. The two-time defending champion is **1-8**; Travon is also **1-8** after seven consecutive losses.

Patrick Mahomes scored **16.48** for Travon and returned after hurting his elbow. Carson Wentz finished at **-2.76**. Chiefs coach Andy Reid described Mahomes as day-to-day Monday, so Travon's quarterback outlook still needs watching.

Jake's win came with a costly finish. Nacua hurt his knee on the Rams' final offensive play; coach Sean McVay says the receiver has MCL and PCL sprains and turf toe and is **likely** headed to injured reserve. That move had not been confirmed in the team report. Burden suffered a concussion and has Chicago's Week 10 bye ahead of him.
