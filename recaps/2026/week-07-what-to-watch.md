# Week 7 Monday Watch: Jake Needs a Barkley Miracle

_The League, 2026. Filed Monday, October 26. Scores and remaining starters checked at 6:25 p.m. Eastern. Dallas at Philadelphia kicks off at 8:15 p.m. ET._

Rob is headed toward 7-0. Devin has already cleared 200. Brian and Eric have commanding leads—but Jake still has one player who could turn an ordinary Monday into league history.

The full accounting can wait for the recap. Tonight is about what's left.

## Jake: One Running Back, 51.78 Points Behind

**Barkley needs more than 51.78 points to deliver Jake's first win.**

Not 51.78 rushing yards. Fantasy points. In this league's **non-PPR scoring**, even 200 scrimmage yards and five touchdowns would produce only 50.

One winning example: **220 scrimmage yards and five touchdowns**, worth 52 points without turnovers. That is the neighborhood Jake needs—not a prediction, but a measure of the assignment.

The first thing to watch is whether Barkley plays at all. He remains **questionable with a hamstring injury**; final game-day availability was not confirmed at this check. Being active would open the door, not establish a normal workload.

Eric can watch without needing another point. Jake needs a performance that makes the entire league watch with him.

## Dave: Pierce Needs the Ball—and Almost Everything Else

**Pierce must outscore Lamb by more than 59.98 points.**

Dave doesn't merely need a career night. He needs one while Brian's remaining receiver contributes little or nothing.

Even **240 scrimmage yards and six touchdowns from Pierce would equal 60 points**—enough only if Lamb finishes below 0.02. Any meaningful Lamb production pushes the assignment higher.

Barkley's availability matters here, too. An absence or limitation could create opportunity for Pierce, though it would not guarantee him the entire backfield. Dave's first rooting interest is opportunity; his second is an Eagles offense that somehow routes practically everything through his running back.

Brian's rooting interest is considerably simpler: CeeDee catches footballs.

## Michael: Ferguson Needs to Beat Goedert by 90.42

**Ferguson must outscore Goedert by more than 90.42 points.**

That is the arithmetic. There is no credible ordinary-football comeback script to attach to it.

Devin's 200.76 already makes this a successful week before Goedert takes the field. Michael needs an unprecedented sort of tight-end explosion while Philadelphia's tight end stays silent. This is effectively settled, subject to corrections and extraordinary events.

## Rob: Protect the Finish, Not the Lead

**Rob leads by 35.24 with three starters left against nobody.**

Travon has no Monday counterpunch. Williams and Aubrey can add points, while Philadelphia's defense gives Rob a reason to enjoy Dallas mistakes—even when his own Cowboys are involved.

The unbeaten start is effectively secure. Tonight determines the finishing margin.

**The watch order:** Barkley's availability first, Jake's miracle bid second, and whether Pierce gets enough opportunity to make Dave's impossible-looking assignment briefly interesting.
