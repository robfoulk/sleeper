# Week 7 Preview: Three on IR, Two Quarterbacks, One Empty Win Column

_The League, 2026. Filed October 20. Current rosters, provisional lineups, and NFL opponents were checked Tuesday evening; injury evidence runs through 10:31 p.m. Eastern. Picks are editorial entertainment._

Rob is 6-0 with Hurts, Adams, and Grupe on IR. Jake is 0-6 after scoring more than everybody except the opponent he drew. Brian is 5-1 without a weekly scoring title, while Michael's 2-4 team has outscored both teams sitting immediately above it.

Week 7 is an excellent time to stop letting the record do all the talking.

Week 7 puts **Buffalo, Jacksonville, the Chargers, and Washington** on bye. That removes Josh Allen from Dave, James Cook and several Jacksonville contributors from Michael, and another chunk of Travon's already depleted lineup. Meanwhile, Eric gets back the players whose absence I thought would sink him last week. He won anyway.

The [Week 6 recap](/sleeper/2026/week-06/) settles my 2-2 prediction record. Here are the next four calls.

## Dave vs Brian: Two Quarterbacks Walk Into the Same Game

**Early and out of hope (3-3) vs Tri-Lams (5-1)**

Buffalo's bye removes Josh Allen from a team on a three-game slide. Early and out of hope would normally take that as permission to update the sign. This time, the replacement plan is more interesting.

The Rams' official Week 7 preview confirms **Kirk Cousins will start for Las Vegas**. Dave can pair him with Matthew Stafford on the other side of the same game. Los Angeles is missing defensive linemen Poona Ford and Kobie Turner, with Myles Garrett still unavailable in the latest NFL report. That is a credible route through the bye, and an exchange of touchdown passes would pay Dave from both ends.

Brian has won three straight, but his scores are 23-19 against all seven weekly opponents, compared with his 5-1 head-to-head record. The standings have rewarded him handsomely. He now needs the available lineup to keep earning it.

D'Andre Swift hosts New England on Thursday, Christian Watson and Matthew Golden visit Detroit, Emeka Egbuka gets Carolina, and Travis Kelce visits Seattle. Those are Dave's supporting routes if the quarterbacks provide a foundation.

The receiving depth has taken a hit: Romeo Doubs is on injured reserve, and Rashee Rice carries a Sleeper out designation. The backfield also needs help, with Jordan Mason on IR and Alvin Kamara questionable on the platform after returning from a back concern Sunday. Kamara's availability remains a watch item.

Brian's roster has its own Buffalo-shaped holes: Keon Coleman, Dalton Kincaid, and Tyler Bass are on bye, as is Justin Herbert with the Chargers. Jordan Love at Detroit, Christian McCaffrey at Atlanta, Nico Collins against the Giants, and CeeDee Lamb at Philadelphia remain a substantial base. Michael Penix is questionable ahead of San Francisco; Cam Ward against Cleveland is another quarterback option on the roster. Evan McPherson at Baltimore can cover a kicker vacancy.

**I pick Dave in a narrow upset.** The confirmed Las Vegas starter makes the second quarterback slot more credible, and the Rams' defensive absences give him an opening. Brian still has McCaffrey, Love, and Collins; a healthy Penix and effective bye replacements could make this call uncomfortable. I'll take Dave to stop the slide at three.

## Rob vs Travon: The Farce Has a Parts Department

**Unstoppable Farce (6-0) vs Von (1-5)**

**Three on IR. Two expected to miss the rest of the season. Zero losses.** Unstoppable Farce has reached the phase where the spare parts have to become permanent.

Hurts is expected to miss the remainder of 2026 while receiving treatment for blood clots; the Eagles and his agent describe a very good prognosis and expect a full recovery. Grupe is done for the season, and Adams faces an expected six-to-ten-week hamstring absence. All three are currently on IR. Trevor Lawrence's Jacksonville bye removes another quarterback option this week.

That gives the October 2 waiver claim for Maye a different weight. He and Caleb Williams are the provisional Thursday pair, playing opposite each other in Chicago. Rob's lowest weekly scoring finish so far is third. The replacement structure has already worked; now it has to last.

Ja'Marr Chase returns from Cincinnati's bye for the trip to Baltimore, Derrick Henry and Zay Flowers face the Bengals, and Kenneth Walker visits Seattle. George Pickens and Brandon Aubrey travel to Philadelphia on Monday. The current provisional lineup includes Tyler Loop at kicker. Josh Jacobs carries a non-injury personal designation, while A.J. Brown is questionable with an ankle tag; both require attention.

Travon has a much longer interruption list. Terry McLaurin is out for the season. Washington's bye also takes Rachaad White away, Jacksonville's removes Brian Thomas, and the Chargers' removes Keaton Mitchell. Quinshon Judkins and David Montgomery carry out designations. Von has become a very short name for a very long replacement list.

Patrick Mahomes at Seattle and Brock Purdy at Atlanta give Travon a credible quarterback pair. Jonathan Taylor visits Minnesota, Trey McBride hosts Denver, and Chris Olave faces Pittsburgh in New Orleans' Paris game. Rome Odunze can give him something to cheer Thursday against New England. The problem is filling the rest of the lineup around them.

**Rob is my pick.** The gap in available depth is too large to ignore. Travon's route starts with his quarterbacks and Taylor delivering big totals; Rob has more places to find an ordinary, useful score. I'll take 7-0 without attaching another unnecessary 200-point promise.

## Devin vs Michael: The Broken Team Would Like a Third Opinion

**Amon Another level (4-2) vs Broken leg broken team (2-4)**

Michael has won two straight, posted the Week 6 high, and made the team name look increasingly out of date. His weekly scoring finishes have climbed from sixth, sixth, fifth, and fifth to fourth and first. Devin opened with three weekly scoring titles in four weeks, then finished fifth and seventh. The records put Devin ahead; the recent scoring direction makes this much less comfortable.

The schedule has inconveniently removed several of the pieces doing the repairing. James Cook is on Buffalo's bye. Jacksonville's takes Bhayshul Tuten, Parker Washington, and Cam Little out of consideration. Breece Hall and Tyler Warren carry out designations. Cook and Tuten just supplied **42.00** of Michael's **196.42**; replacing that production is the central job of the week.

There is still plenty to work with. Jared Goff hosts Green Bay, Tyler Shough faces Pittsburgh in Paris, Chuba Hubbard gets Tampa Bay, Justin Jefferson returns from Minnesota's bye against Indianapolis, and DeVonta Smith hosts Dallas. Michael also has Minnesota's defense available against the Colts. This is a roster with answers, even if several of the questions arrived at once.

Goff adds a useful wrinkle: touchdowns to Devin's Amon-Ra St. Brown would pay both fantasy teams. Michael's quarterback can have a good afternoon while helping the other side stay close. Detroit's choice of scorer will matter almost as much as its total.

Devin gets Amon-Ra St. Brown back against Green Bay and Tee Higgins back at Baltimore. Jaxon Smith-Njigba hosts Kansas City, Bijan Robinson hosts San Francisco, Kyren Williams visits Las Vegas, and George Kittle travels to Atlanta. Mike Evans' Achilles issue leaves his Sunday availability unresolved: Sleeper says out, while Shanahan has left the door open. Lamar Jackson also carries an out tag; Tyler Huntley, Geno Smith, and Aaron Rodgers remain rostered alternatives to pair with Dak Prescott at Philadelphia. Aaron Jones is questionable before Minnesota hosts Indianapolis.

**I pick Devin in the closest call on my card.** The returning receivers and available running-back core give him the edge. Michael's quarterbacks and Jefferson make a third straight win entirely believable. I expect this matchup to demand more from Devin than the 4-2 versus 2-4 records suggest.

## Jake vs Eric: A Vacancy Sign Meets a Returning Cast

**Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast (0-6) vs Boutte on Sunday (3-3)**

Jake has won the last two championships in the archive. This year's business model appears to involve giving everybody else a six-week head start. Week 6 finally produced evidence that the delayed opening might be approaching.

His **171.56** was the week's second-highest score. He would have beaten Eric and every other team except Michael. Malik Nabers, Germie Bernard, Bryce Young, and Chris Boswell supplied meaningful points. The improvement is real; the first win is still missing.

There is also a strange connection to the unbeaten team. Adams' injury hurts Rob, while it could concentrate more Rams receiving work on Jake's Puka Nacua. The same absence stretches the league leader and offers the winless team an opening. Nacua still has to turn that opportunity into yards and touchdowns.

Puka Nacua visits Las Vegas, Ashton Jeanty hosts the Rams, Nabers travels to Houston, and Young gets Tampa Bay. Bo Nix at Arizona and Sam Darnold against Kansas City are quarterback options. Hunter Henry and Luther Burden meet Thursday in Chicago, while Boswell and Bernard face New Orleans in Paris. Jake has enough scoring routes to make an upset plausible.

He also has Saquon Barkley listed out, Baker Mayfield listed out, and Ladd McConkey coming out of a concussion-shortened game. McConkey's team is on bye regardless. San Francisco's defense gets Atlanta after contributing five of the thirty Jake needed Monday.

Eric welcomes back Joe Burrow and Chase Brown at Baltimore, plus Jahmyr Gibbs and Jameson Williams against Green Bay. C.J. Stroud hosts the Giants, Drake London faces San Francisco, and Tucker Kraft visits Detroit. Jayden Daniels' Washington bye takes away last week's 21.00, but Burrow's return supplies a direct quarterback option.

**Eric is my pick, with less confidence than the records suggest.** He won with the headliners away and now gets several of them back. Jake just demonstrated enough scoring to beat him, and Nacua's situation gives that upset route substance. I'll lean toward Boutte on Sunday reaching 4-3; Crazy J's has finally supplied a reason to watch the door.
