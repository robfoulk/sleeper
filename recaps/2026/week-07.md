# Week 7 Recap: One Lap, Seven Wins, No Vacancies

_The League, 2026. Filed October 27. Week 7 ran October 22-26; fantasy results and rosters checked Tuesday morning. Injury evidence covers October 22 through October 27 at 10:10 a.m. Eastern._

**Rob has beaten everybody. Jake has lost to everybody.** Seven weeks have completed the first lap of this eight-team league, and the standings have acquired the uncomfortable authority of a full set of introductions.

Unstoppable Farce finished the circuit **7-0**, with **191.86** against Travon. Brian reached **6-1** with his fourth consecutive win. Devin reclaimed the weekly scoring crown at **205.56**, and Eric's third straight victory moved him into fourth.

There was very little late drama to distribute. The four highest-scoring teams all won, the closest margin was **38.68**, and the average winning margin was **67.31**. Week 7 delivered four separate notices that the meeting could have been an email.

## Brian vs Dave: The Quarterbacks Showed Up. The Upset Didn't.

**Brian 193.28, Dave 124.70. Brian improves to 6-1; Dave falls to 3-4.**

I picked Dave because Kirk Cousins gave him a credible second quarterback alongside Matthew Stafford. That part worked: the pair scored **20.40** and **17.50**, respectively. Brian's Michael Penix and Jordan Love combined for **37.38**, leaving Dave a **0.52-point quarterback advantage** and an enormous problem everywhere else.

Bucky Irving scored **23.00**, Emanuel Wilson and Omar Cooper supplied **17.50 apiece**, and Ollie Gordon added **16.30**. Those four gave Brian **74.30**, enough depth to absorb Nico Collins' **0.60** and Jaylen Waddle's injury-shortened **4.80**. Christian McCaffrey added **16.00** despite briefly leaving San Francisco's loss at Atlanta.

Dave got **15.00** from D'Andre Swift and **10.80** from Dameon Pierce. His other fourteen starters combined for **61.00**. The quarterback plan had a floor; the rest of the building needed work.

Monday began with Brian ahead by **59.98**, Lamb still to play against Pierce. CeeDee scored **19.40**; Pierce answered with **10.80**. The gap grew by another **8.60**.

Brian has won four straight without a weekly scoring title. Dave has lost four straight after opening 3-0. Early and out of hope has now completed both halves of its season-opening promise.

## Rob vs Travon: The Replacement Quarterbacks Are the Foundation

**Rob 191.86, Travon 125.12. Rob reaches 7-0; Travon drops to 1-6.**

Drake Maye scored **27.44** and Caleb Williams **22.12**, putting **49.56** into the two quarterback slots. Maye's three starts for Rob have now yielded **90.70**. The October 2 waiver claim has become one of the season's most consequential transactions.

Javonte Williams supplied **21.50**, Zay Flowers **19.70**, and Josh Jacobs **15.10**. Rob finished third in weekly scoring; his seven finishes are **second, first, second, second, first, third, third**. There is still no bad week to find.

Travon had a useful return from Quinshon Judkins at **22.40**, alongside Trey McBride's **17.10** and Jonathan Taylor's **17.00**. Patrick Mahomes' **6.00** and three zero-point starters left too much uncovered. Von has lost five consecutive games.

The [Monday watch](/sleeper/2026/week-07-what-to-watch/) found Rob ahead by **35.24** with three starters left against nobody. Javonte, Brandon Aubrey's **14.00**, and Philadelphia's **-4.00** added a net **31.50**. Even the Eagles' bad evening came with sufficient padding.

Rob has since released the injured Jalen Hurts and Blake Grupe. Last week's three-IR headline has already become a different roster: Adams remains on IR, while the replacement quarterback pair keeps scoring.

## Devin vs Michael: Pollard Ends the Debate

**Devin 205.56, Michael 110.34. Devin moves to 5-2; Michael slips to 2-5.**

Tony Pollard's **33.50** led the entire matchup. Geno Smith added **23.48**, Jaxon Smith-Njigba **22.30**, and Tee Higgins **20.80**. Those four produced **100.08**, leaving Michael's entire eighteen-man lineup only **10.26** ahead of them.

Devin earned his **fourth weekly scoring title in seven weeks**, rebounding from seventh place in Week 6. Kyren Williams supplied **16.10** and Amon-Ra St. Brown **15.20**. The receiver room resumed normal service.

Michael went from the league's highest score to its lowest, a drop of **86.08**. Jared Goff's **18.02**, Tyler Shough's **13.82**, and Braelon Allen's **13.40** were the leading returns. Justin Jefferson scored **4.40**; Breece Hall and Jake Ferguson each finished at zero.

The bye-week concern in my preview proved substantial: James Cook and Bhayshul Tuten, who had supplied **42.00** in Week 6, were unavailable. Michael's first six scores had all exceeded 150. The seventh stopped at **110.34**, ending his two-game winning streak.

Monday offered no recovery. Devin added **4.80** from Dallas Goedert; Ferguson stayed at zero. I picked Devin in the closest call on my card. He won by **95.22**. Right winner, completely different afternoon.

## Eric vs Jake: A Returning Cast, an Occupied Win Column

**Eric 186.10, Jake 147.42. Eric reaches 4-3; Jake falls to 0-7.**

Joe Burrow returned from his bye with **28.88**, and Jameson Williams brought **21.10**. Tre Tucker's **16.30** and Blake Corum's **16.10** helped turn Eric's returning headliners into a third consecutive win.

Luther Burden led Jake with **19.70**, followed by Kyler Murray's **13.90**, Puka Nacua's **13.80**, and Ashton Jeanty's **13.60**. Bo Nix scored **26.28** on the bench, **20.86** more than starter Bryce Young. That was a meaningful opportunity cost, though the single swap still leaves Eric ahead by **17.82**.

Jake entered Monday **51.78** behind with Barkley left. Saquon returned, scored **13.10**, and left with a back problem. NBC Sports Philadelphia's postgame interview provides the important distinction: Barkley said his hamstring came through well, but his back locked up. His Week 8 availability remains unresolved.

The two-time defending champion has completed a round robin without a win. His last two scoring finishes, second and fifth, have been better than the first five. Crazy J's has improved the breakfast; the first satisfied customer is still waiting.

## Around the League: Returns, Repairs, and a Thursday Deadline

Dave's Friday additions of Tennessee's defense, Carolina's defense, and Zonovan Knight returned **6.00, -3.00, and 6.30**. Brian's addition of AJ Barner produced **4.60**. Jake's late addition of Christian Kirk yielded zero. These were small lineup repairs in a week decided by much larger gaps.

The medical follow-up matters more for the next lap:

Jalen Coker was estimated limited for Carolina. Those were walkthrough estimates; Thursday availability still needs checking.
