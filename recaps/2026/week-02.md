## Game by Game

### Unstoppable Farce (191.48) vs Amon Another level  (183.16)

**Adams Takes the Last Word.**

Rob entered Monday behind 8.68 points, with Adams facing the combined production of Devin's Jaxson Dart and Kyren Williams. Adams delivered 31.50. Dart and Kyren combined for 14.50. That 17-point Monday advantage turned the deficit into an **8.32-point win**, and Rob's 191.48 was the week's highest team total. The original pick landed; the Monday update's caution was justified, but the required Adams night actually arrived.

Devin had plenty of offense worth defending: Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 33.50, Dak Prescott 29.76, and Amon-Ra St. Brown 26.20. Rob answered with Adams and the predicted Ja'Marr Chase rebound, worth 19.50. Neither team reached the preview's ambitious 200-point target. Being right about the winner apparently does not grant credit for the decorative arithmetic.

Dart's 0.80 and Dallas Goedert's 0.40 came in games disrupted by injuries. Better scores on the bench are hindsight alternatives, not proof Devin should have predicted those exits before kickoff. Rob moves to 2-0 with a head-to-head win over Devin; Devin is 1-1 after producing the week's third-highest score.

### Early and out of hope (176.20) vs Broken leg broken team (157.72)

**Out of Hope, In Possession of Two Wins.**

Michael's Monday position was simple and unpleasant: 157.72 points, an 18-point lead, and nobody left to score. Dave still had Matthew Stafford, Cam Skattebo, and Harrison Mevis. Those remaining starters added 36.48, turning Dave's 139.72 into 176.20 and an **18.48-point victory**. Stafford alone supplied 26.98. The upset pick did not survive the evening.

Josh Allen's 40.82 had put Dave within reach in the first place. Together, Allen and Stafford contributed 67.80, while Travis Kelce added 16.10. Michael got 19.90 from James Cook, 17.70 from DeVonta Smith, and 16.70 from Stefon Diggs. That was a useful supporting cast, but not the winning total the preview promised: Michael's score would have beaten Eric or Jake, not the opponent on his schedule.

Dave is 2-0 despite a team name that keeps submitting pessimistic paperwork. Michael is 0-2. The column owns the missed upset; Dave owns the win.

### Von  (189.86) vs Crazy J’s Stable of Stars (113.06)

**The Trade Reunion Comes With a 76.80-Point Receipt.**

Travon did not need Monday to rescue anything. Patrick Mahomes and Brock Purdy combined for 57.46, Jonathan Taylor supplied 25.20, and the New England defense added 23.00. Malachi Fields' 3.00 on Monday completed a 189.86-point week, second only to Rob. Jake finished at 113.06, a **76.80-point defeat**.

The preview's quarterback argument held up. Its smaller trade-reunion bet did too: Trey McBride's 10.10 plus Quentin Johnston's 1.40 beat Ashton Jeanty's 6.30. That is a 5.20-point edge in a much larger team result, not permission to declare the whole trade settled. The quarterbacks, Taylor, and the defense deserve their share of the explanation.

Jake got 24.08 from Bryce Young and 15.50 from Omarion Hampton, but Malik Nabers supplied only 0.10, and the Rams defense's 12.00 could not repair the deficit. The new Crazy J's nameplate has plenty of showroom confidence; the standings are still asking for the first sale. Travon is 1-1, while Jake is 0-2.

### Tri-Lams (170.98) vs Sanders Boutte on Sunday (131.22)

**Brian Declines the Invitation to Be Upset.**

The original preview wanted Eric's passing-game pieces to join Jahmyr Gibbs in a broader rebound. Brian instead brought CeeDee Lamb's 27.30, Christian McCaffrey's 18.60, and Dalton Kincaid's 15.50 to a 170.98-point win. Eric finished at 131.22. The **39.76-point margin** leaves little room to appeal the second failed upset pick.

Monday's required miracle was specific: Blake Corum needed to outscore Isaiah Likely by more than 45.66. Corum scored 9.20 and Likely 3.30, a 5.90-point improvement for Eric that changed the margin without changing the outcome. Gibbs' 17.30 and Joe Burrow's 16.18 led Eric's starters; Jayden Daniels' elbow injury adds a separate concern for the coming week, not a reason to rewrite this result.

Brian joins Rob and Dave at 2-0. Eric joins Michael and Jake at 0-2. Tri-Lams has two wins, and the column has exhausted its Week 2 upset budget.
