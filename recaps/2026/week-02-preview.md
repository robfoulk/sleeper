# Week 2 Preview: Receipts, Rematches, and Reckless Confidence

_The League, 2026. Filed September 15. Roster and availability snapshot: 15:17 UTC (11:17 a.m. Eastern). NFL Week 2 runs September 17-21._

One week in, and the league already has a high-score race, an injured star changing hands, three updated nameplates, and a trade that gets its own head-to-head reunion. Nobody has earned certainty. Everybody has earned an opinion.

The facts below are checked. **The picks are for fun.** These are new Week 2 calls, not edits to the preliminary draft or anyone's earlier predictions. Four winners, two upset picks, and enough confidence to make next Tuesday uncomfortable.

## The Main Event: Rob vs Devin

**Unstoppable Farce (1-0) vs Amon Another level (1-0)**

Devin scored 210.80. Rob scored 208.88. The 1.92-point difference was smaller than the score of several players their owners would happily have forgotten. Now the schedule puts the two highest-scoring teams in the same game. Excellent timing; absolutely terrible conditions for either owner's humility.

**Why Devin wins:** Bijan Robinson gets Carolina, whose own September 14 review acknowledged allowing Chicago 291 rushing yards in Week 1. That is an inviting matchup, not a promise to reproduce it. Justin Jefferson faces Chicago, Amon-Ra St. Brown visits Buffalo on Thursday, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba goes to Arizona. Devin has several ways to make Rob answer a large number before Monday.

**Why Rob wins:** Williams and Henry combined for 71.56 last week, Kenneth Walker added 31.10, and Chase scored only 1.20. The fun case is that Rob has already shown the ceiling without getting anything resembling a normal Chase afternoon. Henry gets New Orleans, Williams faces Minnesota, and Walker's Kansas City team hosts Indianapolis. There is plenty here without pretending Brown can help this week.

**The lineup wrinkle:** Brown is on IR. Bowers and Flowers carry out tags, while Coker is questionable after rolling his ankle during Sunday's 25.80-point performance. Rob has Mayer as a rostered tight-end contingency and Adams among his healthy-tagged receivers. Coker is a real decision only if available, not an automatic reward for last week's bench points.

**The call: Rob.** Devin wins the opening-week scoring crown; Rob wins the first direct argument. The prediction is a rebound from Chase, not another requirement for three 30-point performances. Devin is welcome to have Bijan make this paragraph look ridiculous.

## The Upset Desk: Michael vs Dave

**Broken leg broken team (0-1) vs Early and out of hope (1-0)**

Dave cleared 200 with Allen, Swift, and Watson combining for 93.76. Michael scored 169.18, traded Brown for TeSlaa and a pick upgrade, and now has to make the present work while the future picks sit there being extremely unhelpful on Sundays.

**Dave's route:** Allen opens the week against Detroit on Thursday. Swift faces Minnesota, Watson visits the Jets, and Stafford gets the Giants on Monday. Stafford's Week 1 total was only 4.10, so there is room for Dave to improve without demanding that the first three repeat their exact scores. Mason's questionable thumb tag deserves attention; Tyrone Tracy is already on Dave's roster as a running-back alternative.

**Michael's route:** Lamar Jackson hosts New Orleans, Hubbard visits Atlanta, and Breece Hall hosts Green Bay. James Cook gets Buffalo's Thursday game. Those are actual options on Michael's current roster, not players credited to him because an old article remembers them there. At receiver, Vele and Diggs offer alternatives while Pierce's hand status remains unresolved; TeSlaa is a developmental option, not a guaranteed replacement for Brown's role.

**The call: Michael, upset No. 1.** The pick is a broader supporting performance around Lamar, while Dave discovers that a team name containing "out of hope" has a contractual obligation to cause at least one unnecessary crisis. Dave has the stronger Week 1 result. We are picking the plot twist anyway.

## The Trade Reunion: Jake vs Travon

**Performance Anxiety (0-1) vs Von (0-1)**

The August 31 deal sent Jeanty to Jake and McBride plus Johnston to Travon, with a 2027 eighth going to Travon and a twelfth going to Jake. Week 1: Jeanty 26.70, McBride and Johnston 17.20 combined. Week 2: the owners get to watch their former assets while playing each other. No extra features required; the schedule has supplied the entertainment.

**Jake's route:** Jeanty visits the Chargers, Barkley visits Tennessee, and Puka Nacua plays the Giants on Monday. McConkey is questionable with a chest designation, so Jake needs a real receiver contingency. Malik Nabers is on his roster and also plays in that Monday game; Michael Pittman is another rostered receiver option. Exact start choices depend on availability, not on trying to recover all 58.10 points from last week's defeat at once.

**Travon's route:** Mahomes faces Indianapolis, Purdy hosts Miami, and McBride hosts Seattle. Travon led the losing teams in Week 1 scoring, and his quarterback pair supplied 42.76. McLaurin and Johnston produced just 3.10 combined; even a modest recovery there helps. Brian Thomas' questionable shoulder tag and Kyler Murray's out/concussion designation remain availability concerns, not reasons to erase the productive players around them.

**The call: Travon.** McBride gets the trade-reunion spotlight, the quarterback pair does enough, and Jake spends another week insisting the roster looks better than the standings. A tie is possible, but this column is not spending its one chance at confidence predicting paperwork.

## The Other Upset: Eric vs Brian

**Sanders Boutte on Sunday (0-1) vs Tri-Lams (1-0)**

Brian beat Michael with depth; Eric lost to Devin despite Gibbs' 28.60. The lazy preview would stop there and pick Brian. The entertaining version asks what happens when Eric's quarterbacks and receivers remember they are invited, too.

**Brian's route:** Likely gets the Rams on Monday after leading his Week 1 starters with 19.80. Love visits the Jets, Herbert hosts Las Vegas, and McCaffrey hosts Miami. The lineup does not need one player to dominate. It needs another broad contribution like the one that produced 208.54. Henderson is provisionally in the upcoming starter list but still carries an out tag. That slot needs attention; Bucky Irving, McCaffrey, Etienne, and Emmett Johnson are rostered alternatives, subject to who already occupies the other running-back slots.

**Eric's route:** Gibbs visits Buffalo on Thursday, Daniels visits Dallas, and Burrow goes to Houston. Drake London gets Carolina. A better collective showing from that group is a perfectly coherent path to an upset, even if Week 1 did not provide it. Eric has no injury designations in this captured roster snapshot; that is a cleaner planning sheet, not a guarantee everyone is medically healthy.

**The call: Eric, upset No. 2.** Gibbs sets the tone on Thursday, the passing-game pieces join in, and Brian discovers that last week's beautifully balanced score cannot be submitted twice. Brian remains the entirely defensible pick. It is simply not this column's pick.
