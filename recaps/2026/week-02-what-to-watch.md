## Michael vs Dave: The Upset Pick Meets Three Remaining Starters

**Original pick: Michael. Monday watch: Dave needs more than 18.00 combined points from Stafford, Skattebo, and Mevis.**

Michael's 157.72 is in the bank. James Cook supplied 19.90, DeVonta Smith 17.70, and Stefon Diggs 16.70, but there is nobody left in his starting lineup to answer. Dave has Josh Allen's 40.82 keeping the target manageable and three different ways to collect the rest.

Watch Stafford's red-zone possessions. Passing touchdowns help Dave directly; stalled drives can still produce Mevis field goals. Skattebo supplies a separate route through the Giants' offense. An 18-point lead looks less comfortable when the other side still has a quarterback, running back, and kicker. The original Michael upset call stays put; Dave is now the better-positioned side to finish 2-0 and leave Michael at 0-2.

## Devin vs Rob: Adams Needs More Than a Good Night

**Original pick: Rob. Monday watch: Adams must outscore Dart and Kyren combined by more than 8.68.**

The Chase rebound part of the original argument arrived: 19.50 points after the preview cited his 1.20-point opener. But Devin answered with 33.50 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba, 29.76 from Dak Prescott, and 26.20 from Amon-Ra St. Brown. Rob has not merely fallen behind a stationary target; the leader still has two starters running.

Watch who finishes Rams drives. An Adams touchdown moves Rob toward the comeback. A Kyren rushing touchdown moves Devin farther away while taking a scoring opportunity off Adams' plate. Dart's passing and rushing production can keep raising the bar from the other sideline. Devin has the stronger position in this meeting of 1-0 teams; Rob needs a concentrated Adams scoring night, not just a respectable receiving line.

The original bold call that **both teams clear 200** is still open, but demanding: Rob needs more than 40.02 from Adams, while Devin needs more than 31.34 combined from Dart and Kyren. Those are thresholds, not forecasts.

## Brian vs Eric: An Upset Pick in Serious Trouble

**Original pick: Eric. Monday watch: Corum must outscore Likely by more than 45.66.**

CeeDee Lamb's 27.30 and Christian McCaffrey's 18.60 helped Brian build a commanding lead. Eric got 17.30 from Gibbs, but the broader upset has not materialized. A normal productive Corum outing is nowhere near enough, and every Likely point adds to the requirement.

Watch Corum's share of carries and goal-line work for what it says about his future role, as well as tonight's remote comeback path. Brian is firmly positioned for 2-0; Eric needs an extraordinary outlier to avoid 0-2. The September 15 Eric pick does not get quietly exchanged for Brian now.

## Travon vs Jake: The Quarterback Argument Holds Up

**Original pick: Travon. Monday watch: Nabers and the Rams defense must outscore Fields by more than 85.90 for Jake to win.**

Travon's quarterback pair delivered exactly the kind of support the original preview anticipated: Patrick Mahomes and Brock Purdy combined for 57.46, with Jonathan Taylor adding 25.20. Travon has a commanding route to his first win; Jake is facing a huge deficit even before Fields adds anything.

Puka Nacua is currently on Jake's bench, not in the remaining-starter total. His availability is still worth tracking, but bench points do not change this matchup.

The separate McBride-plus-Johnston versus Jeanty bold prediction remains exactly as filed in the original preview. This update does not rewrite it or substitute a different trade comparison.
