# Week 12 Preview: A One-Game Grip on Fourth

_The League, 2026. Ownership, Week 12 pairings, and Sleeper tags checked Tuesday, November 24, at 5:11 p.m. Eastern; practice reporting reviewed through 5:48 p.m. Lineups and availability can still change._

Four regular-season weeks remain before the Week 16 semifinals. Rob (10-1), Brian (9-2), Devin (7-4), and Eric (6-5) currently hold the four playoff places. Michael and Dave are each one win behind Eric. Jake and Travon are 1-10; even four straight wins would leave them with five, short of the six wins Eric already has. They can still play spoiler, but the playoff race has narrowed to the other six teams. Week 12 opens Wednesday with Green Bay at the Rams, then runs through Thanksgiving and the weekend slate.

## Devin vs Dave: Dave's Chance to Reach the Cut Line

**Amon Another level (7-4) vs Early and out of hope (5-6).** Dave can reach 6-6 with a win and pull level with Eric by record if Rob beats Eric. Devin has scored **2,092.02** points, **344.90** more than Dave. Dak Prescott gets Philadelphia at home, Amon-Ra St. Brown hosts Chicago, and George Kittle gets Seattle in San Francisco. Dave counters with Cam Skattebo at Indianapolis and Travis Kelce at Buffalo.

Tuesday's Sleeper snapshot listed Devin's Dallas Goedert as questionable with a shin issue and Dave's Sam LaPorta as questionable with an ankle issue. Detroit's Tuesday walkthrough estimate listed LaPorta as a full participant after Monday's limited estimate. It's encouraging for Dave, though it does not confirm game-day availability.

**Pick: Devin.** The better record and 344.90-point scoring lead make him the safer call. LaPorta's full-practice estimate gives Dave a credible path, but he still needs production from LaPorta and Kelce.

## Rob vs Eric: First Place Meets the Cut Line

**Unstoppable Farce (10-1) vs Boutte on Sunday (6-5).** Rob leads the league in points for at **2,166.32**; Eric has **1,927.24** and a one-win hold on fourth. A loss could leave him level with Michael or Dave if either wins. Rob's Derrick Henry visits Houston and Jahmyr Gibbs hosts Chicago. Eric has Joe Burrow against New Orleans and Jameson Williams against the Bears on Thanksgiving.

Sleeper's snapshot lists Henry as questionable with a concussion after his Week 11 evaluation; NFL.com reported he told ESPN he felt fine afterward. The sources also differ on Jalen Coker: Sleeper still lists him as questionable, while Carolina said Monday that head coach Dave Canales reported Coker had cleared concussion protocol and was good to go.

**Pick: Rob.** His 10-1 record and league-leading scoring make him the pick. Eric has a real route to an upset through Burrow and the Thanksgiving matchup, with fourth place adding urgency.

## Jake vs Brian: Brian Tries to Keep Pace

**Crazy J’s Bed & Breakfast (1-10) vs Tri-Lams (9-2).** Brian is eight wins ahead and has scored **519.16** more points than Jake. Jake's Saquon Barkley visits Dallas and Malik Nabers visits Indianapolis. Brian's Jordan Love visits the Rams Wednesday; CeeDee Lamb hosts Philadelphia on Thanksgiving.

In Tuesday's Sleeper snapshot, Barkley is questionable with an arm issue, Kincaid is listed out with a thumb issue, and DJ Moore is questionable with a knee issue. NBC Sports reported that Kincaid returned to a limited Tuesday session and told reporters he was most likely to play Thursday. His availability still needs confirmation.

**Pick: Brian.** The record and season-long scoring gap favor him, even with Moore uncertain and Kincaid's status unresolved. Jake needs Barkley available and a ceiling week from Nabers to make Brian work for it.

## Michael vs Travon: Two Quarterbacks, One Thanksgiving Game

**Broken leg broken team (5-6) vs Von (1-10).** Michael is one win behind fourth-place Eric and **377.80** points ahead of Travon. Josh Allen and James Cook host Kansas City on Thanksgiving; Travon's Patrick Mahomes goes to Buffalo. Michael has Jacksonville's Bhayshul Tuten and kicker Cam Little at home against Tennessee.

Sleeper's snapshot lists both Tuten and Little as questionable. It also lists Juwan Johnson as questionable with a hamstring issue for New Orleans' Sunday game at Cincinnati, and Keaton Mitchell as questionable with a back issue before the Chargers host New England.

**Pick: Michael.** Allen and Cook give him two pieces of the same Thanksgiving matchup, while Mahomes gives Travon a dangerous answer. Thursday's quarterback showdown could set the tone for the week.
