# Week 8 Preview: Everybody Gets a Second Look

_The League, 2026. Filed October 27. Rosters, pairings, and NFL opponents checked Tuesday morning; injury evidence through 10:10 a.m. Eastern._

**The first lap is complete. Week 8 repeats the Week 1 pairings**, with seven weeks of evidence available to complicate the introductions.

Rob gets the defending champion again, now with **7-0 against 0-7** attached. Brian faces Michael with McCaffrey, Nico Collins, and Houston's defense on bye. Dave and Travon meet carrying a combined nine consecutive losses. Devin and Eric supply the heavyweight game: four weekly scoring titles against a three-game winning streak.

**Houston, the Giants, New Orleans, and San Francisco are on bye.** Carolina visits Green Bay on Thursday; Chicago visits Seattle on Monday. Thursday puts several early decisions directly in front of owners who would appreciate another day.

## Dave vs Travon: Somebody Has to Stop the Slide

**Early and out of hope (3-4) vs Von (1-6)**
**Week 1: Dave 201.06, Travon 176.56.**

Dave has lost four straight. Travon has lost five. Their first meeting produced **377.62** combined points; their Week 7 scores combined for **249.82**. The rematch arrives with rather different advertising.

Josh Allen returns from Buffalo's bye to host Baltimore, giving Dave a route back to his Allen-Stafford pair. Stafford hosts the Chargers; Kirk Cousins, whom Las Vegas has confirmed will start again, visits the Jets. His **20.40** last week made the replacement plan respectable. Allen gives Dave a chance to raise the ceiling.

Omarion Hampton returns from the Chargers' bye for the trip to the Rams, while D'Andre Swift visits Seattle. Michael Wilson gets Dallas. Christian Watson and Matthew Golden host Carolina on Thursday, but Golden's shoulder/foot concern made him an estimated non-participant at Monday's walkthrough. Dave cannot plan around a confirmed return from Emeka Egbuka, who carries a concussion/out tag, or Rashee Rice, still listed out with a hamstring issue.

New Orleans' bye removes Alvin Kamara and Kendre Miller. Cam Skattebo's hamstring concern comes with a Giants bye. Dave has quarterbacks to spare, but useful running-back and receiver slots still need attention.

Travon loses Brock Purdy and Chris Olave to byes. David Montgomery's Houston bye takes him out regardless of his groin designation. Patrick Mahomes visits Denver after scoring **6.00** against Seattle; Jacoby Brissett at Dallas is a rostered second-quarterback option.

There are returning pieces. Brian Thomas hosts Indianapolis, and Rachaad White gets Philadelphia. Jonathan Taylor faces Jacksonville, Judkins visits Pittsburgh, and Trey McBride gets Dallas. Travon also has Keaton Mitchell and Quentin Johnston against the Rams. Rashid Shaheed remains a question: Sleeper lists him out with a knee concern, while Mike Macdonald described encouraging early returns. That does not confirm Monday availability.

**I pick Dave.** Allen's return and the available quarterback depth give him the more convincing repair kit. Travon's path runs through Taylor, Judkins, McBride, and a much better Mahomes game. Early and out of hope gets a week in which the sign can be turned around.

## Michael vs Brian: The Bye Week Moves Across the Table

**Broken leg broken team (2-5) vs Tri-Lams (6-1)**
**Week 1: Brian 208.54, Michael 169.18.**

Michael spent Week 7 trying to replace Cook and Tuten. Brian now gets the same exercise with McCaffrey and Collins.

Cook hosts Baltimore and Tuten hosts Indianapolis. They supplied **42.00** in Michael's Week 6 win before their byes. Jared Goff and Justin Jefferson share Minnesota at Detroit, giving Michael meaningful interests on both sides. DK Metcalf hosts Cleveland, while Parker Washington returns against Indianapolis.

Shough's New Orleans bye requires a quarterback change. Deshaun Watson at Pittsburgh is currently available on the roster beside Goff. Breece Hall and Tyler Warren still carry out designations, and DeVonta Smith is listed out with a hamstring concern. Their availability could change the depth calculation; none is a promised rescue.

Brian loses McCaffrey, Collins, Isaiah Likely, Ka'imi Fairbairn, and Houston's defense to the bye schedule, with Deebo Samuel and Travis Etienne also unavailable regardless of their injury tags. McCaffrey's diagnosis needs care: Shanahan described a mild hamstring strain, while Sleeper lists calf. The 49ers are optimistic about him after the bye.

Brian's remaining foundation is substantial. Jordan Love hosts Carolina on Thursday; Justin Herbert returns against the Rams, and Penix visits Tampa Bay. Bucky Irving plays Atlanta, Lamb hosts Arizona, and Emanuel Wilson gets Chicago on Monday. Irving, Wilson, Omar Cooper, and Ollie Gordon combined for **74.30** last week. That is a strong answer to any claim that Tri-Lams runs entirely through McCaffrey.

Waddle's chest injury leaves a receiver decision unresolved ahead of Kansas City's visit. Dalton Kincaid has a platform ribs/questionable tag. Tyler Bass returns to cover a kicker slot, but Brian needs another defense beyond Seattle; Houston's bye leaves a clear opening to fill.

**I pick Michael in a narrow upset.** Cook and Tuten restore two productive slots while Brian replaces several. Michael's **110.34** last week deserves weight, but so do the six scores above 150 that preceded it. Brian can make this pick look foolish through exactly the depth that just beat Dave. I'll take the bye-week reversal and make him prove it again.

## Rob vs Jake: Seven Answers Against Seven Questions

**Unstoppable Farce (7-0) vs Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast (0-7)**
**Week 1: Rob 208.88, Jake 150.78.**

Rob's worst scoring finish is third. Jake's best scoring finish is second, and that week still ended in a loss. The rematch carries a stark record gap with scoring evidence behind it.

Maye visits Miami and Caleb Williams visits Seattle. Trevor Lawrence returns against Indianapolis as another quarterback option. Jacobs hosts Carolina on Thursday; Chase gets Tennessee, Javonte Williams and Pickens get Arizona, and Garrett Wilson hosts Las Vegas. Henry and Flowers visit Buffalo.

That is a wide spread of available contributors. Rob also gets Bowers against the Jets and Loveland against Seattle. Coker's shoulder concern needs an early decision after an estimated limited Monday walkthrough. Adams remains on IR; Rob has released Hurts and Grupe as the replacements become permanent.

Jake has quarterback choices worth revisiting. Nix hosts Kansas City after scoring **26.28** on the bench, Mayfield returns against Atlanta, and Darnold hosts Chicago on Monday. Young visits Green Bay on Thursday; his full-participation entry Monday was an estimate after a walkthrough.

Barkley faces Washington if available. In a postgame interview published Tuesday, he said his back locked up Monday while his hamstring came through well. His workload and availability remain the central question. Jeanty gets the Jets, Nacua hosts the Chargers, and Burden travels to Seattle after leading Jake with **19.70**.

The byes take Nabers, Woody Marks, Christian Kirk, and Kaelon Black out of consideration. McConkey remains listed out after his concussion even as the Chargers return. Gainwell has a hip/questionable tag. Jake released San Francisco's defense Tuesday and currently has only the Rams rostered at that position; a second defense is an immediate job.

**I pick Rob.** Jake can improve his quarterback return and still need a much healthier supporting lineup. Nacua, Burden, and a productive Barkley would give the upset a shape. Rob has supplied a top-three score every week; that is the stronger case for 8-0.

## Devin vs Eric: The Best Rematch Is the Least Comfortable Pick

**Amon Another level (5-2) vs Boutte on Sunday (4-3)**
**Week 1: Devin 210.80, Eric 163.02.**

Devin leads Eric in season scoring by **149.88**. Eric has won three straight. Their last result was a **47.78-point** Devin win, but the available Week 8 lineups make a repeat much less obvious.

Pollard visits Cincinnati after his **33.50**; Bijan Robinson visits Tampa Bay, and Kyren Williams hosts the Chargers. St. Brown gets Minnesota, Higgins gets Tennessee, and Smith-Njigba hosts Chicago. Those three receivers just combined for **58.30**. Devin has plenty of reasons to expect another serious total.

Prescott hosts Arizona, Geno Smith gets Las Vegas, and Rodgers faces Cleveland. Lamar Jackson is still listed out by Sleeper; Jesse Minter expressed optimism Monday but said Baltimore would know more Wednesday. A return against Buffalo is possible, not confirmed.

San Francisco's bye removes Kittle and Pineiro, plus Evans regardless of his Achilles designation. Goedert faces Washington, with Darren Waller at Green Bay and Kenyon Sadiq against Las Vegas among the tight-end alternatives. There is only one rostered kicker with a game, Reichard at Detroit. Jones is day to day with a knee concern, and St. Brown's platform ankle tag requires monitoring.

Eric loses Stroud to Houston's bye, but Jayden Daniels returns to host Philadelphia. Burrow gets Tennessee after scoring **28.88**. Malik Willis against New England supplies another quarterback choice.

Gibbs and Jameson Williams host Minnesota, Chase Brown gets Tennessee, Corum plays the Chargers, and RJ Harvey hosts Kansas City. Tucker Kraft starts the fantasy week against Carolina on Thursday. Jakobi Meyers and Brenton Strange return against Indianapolis, giving Eric options beyond last week's starters.

London gets Tampa Bay after returning from Sunday's concussion evaluation. Sleeper still carries a head/questionable designation; watch the follow-up. Campbell's statement that Gibbs "should be good" is encouraging, with the practice week still ahead. Eric's three rostered defenses all have games, including Jacksonville against Indianapolis, so he can reconsider Baltimore's trip to Buffalo.

**I pick Eric, narrowly.** Daniels' return and a roster with fewer mandatory bye repairs give him the edge in this week's lineup problem. Devin's season-long scoring advantage makes this the pick most likely to hurt. If Pollard and the receivers bring another hundred-point core, Eric will need his returning options immediately.

## The Early Decisions

**Thursday: Carolina at Green Bay.** Jacobs, Love, Kraft, Watson, Golden, Young, and Coker put both productive starters and unresolved availability on the early deadline. Green Bay's banged-up offensive line also bears watching.

**Before Sunday:** Brian needs a second defense, Jake needs one, and Devin needs another active kicker. Those vacant bye-week slots can undo several good player choices.

**Monday: Chicago at Seattle.** Caleb Williams, Burden, Swift, Odunze, Smith-Njigba, Darnold, Loveland, and Emanuel Wilson could leave useful scoring on the final night. Whether any matchup deserves suspense will depend on the actual gaps.
